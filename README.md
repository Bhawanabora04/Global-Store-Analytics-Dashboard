# 🌍 Global Store Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Store Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze global sales, revenue, profit, loss, customers, products, discounts, and regional performance.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance and helps identify trends across different countries, cities, customer segments, products, and regions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Track total customers and orders.
- Analyze revenue, profit, and loss.
- Monitor profit percentage.
- Identify revenue and profit trends over time.
- Analyze revenue by country and city.
- Compare customer segment performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze product-wise discounts.
- Compare regional and sub-regional revenue performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Data Visualization**
- **Interactive Filters & Slicers**
- **Map Visualizations**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Overview

The dashboard provides key business metrics including:

- **Total Customers:** 17K
- **Total Orders:** 26K
- **Total Revenue:** 12.64M
- **Total Profit:** 1.47M
- **Total Loss:** -920.65K
- **Profit %:** 0.12

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.

---

### 2. Revenue & Profit Trends

The dashboard includes time-series analysis of:

- Revenue over time
- Profit over time

This helps identify changes and trends in business performance across different years.

---

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis

Revenue is analyzed across different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This helps compare the contribution of each customer segment to overall revenue.

---

### 4. Geographic Analysis

The dashboard provides geographic analysis using:

- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by City
- Regional revenue
- Sub-regional revenue

Map visualizations help understand the geographic distribution of sales.

---

### 5. Top Products Analysis

The dashboard identifies the top-performing products based on revenue.

This helps understand which products contribute significantly to overall business revenue.

---

### 6. Discount Analysis

The dashboard includes product-wise discount analysis to compare discount levels across different products.

This can help explore the relationship between discounting and business performance.

---

### 7. Regional Performance

Revenue is analyzed across different regions and sub-regions to identify variations in business performance across geographic markets.

---
